Welcome Aboard

Arcturus+ is a Hong Kong-based sailing race team competing in local harbour races and offshore passages across SE Asia — Taiwan, Japan, Vietnam, Philippines and beyond.

This site shares our team's insights, lessons learned, and race reports. Whether you're a crew member or a fellow sailor, we invite comments and collaboration from around the world.
