Tide

Tidal prediction, current assessment, and timing strategy for HK and SE Asia waters.

Key Concepts

  • Tidal heights — HW/LW times and heights
  • Tidal streams — direction and rate
  • Neap vs Spring — impact on current strength
  • HK Harbour tidal patterns — ~1.5m range typical

Tide Apps & Sources

  • Hong Kong Tide Tables — Marine Department
  • navily.com — tide overlay on charts
  • Windy — tidal stream visualization

Passage Timing

Use tide tables to time departures for favourable currents. Against current can cost 1-2kts boat speed.

Our Practice

Always check tide before race start — HW morning vs HW afternoon changes strategy significantly.
