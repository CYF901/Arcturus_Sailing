Food Arrangement

Provisioning, meal planning, galley management for offshore races.

Provisioning Principles

  • Calorie-dense food for physical demands
  • Easy to prepare under motion
  • Minimal freshwater usage
  • No perishables on long passages

Sample Offshore Menu

  • Breakfast: Oats, Udon noodles, energy bars
  • Lunch: Rice balls, canned fish, trail mix
  • Dinner: Instant noodles, dehydrated meals, tinned food
  • Drinks: Water, electrolytes, tea, coffee
