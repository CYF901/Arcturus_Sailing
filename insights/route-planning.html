Route Planning

Passage planning for offshore races — weather routing, waypoint selection, timing.

Our Key Routes

  • HK → Keelung (Taiwan): ~530NM, 2-3 days, via Taiwan Strait
  • HK → Okinawa (Japan): ~750NM, 4-5 days
  • HK → Vietnam: ~650NM, 3-4 days
  • HK → Philippines: ~600NM, 3-4 days

Weather Window Selection

  • NE Monsoon (Oct-Mar): Best for Taiwan/Japan/Philippines
  • SW Monsoon (Jun-Sep): Risk of typhoons — avoid or careful timing
  • Plan for 30% time buffer on longer legs
