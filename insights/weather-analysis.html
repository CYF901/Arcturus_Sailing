Voyage Weather Intelligence

This dashboard dynamically queries Open-Meteo to build a customized forecast along your exact route. By estimating your vessel's progress, it projects the weather you will encounter at the specific time you arrive at each coordinate.

⚠️

Navigational Disclaimer: This application provides meteorological forecasts based strictly on your estimated straight-line progress. It explicitly does not consider ocean currents, tidal streams, or vessel leeway. Always consult local tide tables, pilot charts, and current models alongside this data.

Terms of Use: Data provided is for informational purposes only and is generated by third-party APIs. Use at your own risk. Do not rely solely on this dashboard for life-safety or critical navigational decisions.

Data Sources: Routing forecasts via Open-Meteo | Radar via RainViewer | Synoptic models via PredictWind.
