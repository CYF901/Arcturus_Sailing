Weather Analysis

Weather patterns, forecasting tools, and typhoon avoidance strategies for SE Asia.

Regional Weather Patterns

  • NE Monsoon: Oct-Apr, N-NE winds 10-25kt, dry conditions
  • SW Monsoon: May-Sep, SW-W winds, hot and humid, typhoon season
  • Inter-monsoon: Apr-May and Sep-Oct, light and variable

Forecasting Tools

  • Windy.com — primary GRIB weather tool
  • Typhoon2000 — tropical cyclone tracking
  • NOAA GFS — 10-day global model
  • ECMWF — better for typhoon tracking

Typhoon Protocol

  • Monitor all systems within 1000km of route
  • Always have an escape plan / alter course options
  • No sailing inside a typhoon warning area
  • Seek shelter early — don't wait
