Marine Currents & Tidal Streams

Understanding the interplay between large-scale ocean currents (like the Kuroshio) and local tidal streams is critical for safe and optimal navigation around Taiwan. Below are direct answers to your fundamental queries.

➤ Vector Combination

Q: Is the speed and direction showing the ocean current a combined vector for ocean current and tidal stream?

Yes. Most modern marine forecasting tools (like HYCOM or Copernicus) that display "Total Surface Current" show the kinematic vector sum.

If an ocean current flows North at 1.5 knots, and the tidal stream is flowing South at 1.0 knots, the combined vector will show North at 0.5 knots. Conversely, when the tide turns North, the combined vector becomes 2.5 knots North.

⌛ Update Frequency

Q: Is the update frequency enough to show the change of tidal stream?

Yes, absolutely. High-resolution numerical models covering the Taiwan coast provide data at an hourly resolution.

Since tides in this region are primarily mixed, dominant semi-diurnal (changing direction approximately every 6 hours), an hourly update frequency is highly capable of capturing the acceleration, peak, and slack water phases of the tidal streams.

May 30 – June 4, 2026

Delivery: Hong Kong to Keelung

Sailing northeast through the Taiwan Strait. In early June, the southwest monsoon begins to establish, generally creating a favorable northward surface current.

⚠ Key Hazards

  • High commercial traffic in the Strait.
  • Squalls associated with the transitioning monsoon season.
  • Strong local tidal streams accelerating around headlands.

📐 Current Strategy

  • Stay slightly offshore (West coast) to ride the prevailing northward current.
  • Time the rounding of the northern cape (Fuguijiao) with a favorable tidal stream.

Authoritative Data Sources & References

The mathematical vector model visualized in the map above is an offline, simplified demonstration of how ocean currents and tidal streams interact based on historical profiles. For actual race routing and live data downloads, please consult the authoritative operational forecasting centers below:

  • 🔗
    Taiwan Central Weather Administration (CWA) - Marine Forecast

    Provides high-resolution local model data (hourly updates) for tidal streams and wave heights directly around Taiwan, Penghu, and Keelung.

  • 🔗
    Copernicus Marine Service

    Global and regional ocean physical analysis and forecasting. Excellent for observing the larger Kuroshio vector before it combines with local tides.

  • 🔗
    Windy.com - Ocean Currents Overlay

    A highly accessible visualization tool that integrates various models (like ECMWF) to show the kinematic vector sum of currents and tides on an hourly basis.
