Boat Maintenance

Pre-race checks, post-race maintenance, and seasonal upkeep.

Pre-Race Checklist

  • Engine — oil level, coolant, raw water filter
  • Electrical — battery voltage, connections, nav lights
  • Hull — through-hulls, rudder, seacocks
  • Standing rigging — turnbuckles, cotter pins
  • Sails — battens, seams, hardware
  • Safety gear — flares, lifejackets, PLB, EPIRB

Post-Race Maintenance

  • Rinse sails in fresh water
  • Check for chafe points
  • Engine impeller inspection
  • Standing rigging tension check

Seasonal Maintenance

  • Antifoul — bottom paint schedule
  • Standing rigging replacement schedule
  • Standing rigging replacement schedule
  • Safety equipment servicing dates
